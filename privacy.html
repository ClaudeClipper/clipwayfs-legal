3. How We Use Your Data

We use your data exclusively for:

  • Authenticating with TikTok's Content Posting API
  • Uploading video content to your TikTok account as instructed by you
  • Generating captions, hashtags, and metadata for uploaded content

We do not sell, share, or transfer your data to third parties beyond what is necessary to provide the Service (i.e., transmitting content to TikTok via their official API).

4. Data Storage and Security

  • OAuth tokens and credentials are stored locally on your device and are never transmitted to our servers.
  • Video content is processed locally and uploaded directly to TikTok's servers via their API.
  • We do not operate central servers that store your personal data.
  • We implement reasonable technical measures to protect the data processed by the application.

5. Data Retention

Authentication tokens are stored only for the duration needed to maintain your TikTok API session. You can revoke access at any time by removing the application's authorization in your TikTok account settings, which invalidates all stored tokens.
